# AgentDock Target Audience

Updated: 6 May 2026

## Primary Buyer

Head of Customer Service, Service Manager, or Operations Lead at a managed connectivity, telecom, broadband, utilities, housing, facilities, or field-service business.

They manage work that crosses departments:

- Customer support
- Complaints
- Finance / billing
- Field operations
- Account management
- Compliance / QA

## What They Want

They want fewer surprises:

- Fewer avoidable SLA breaches
- Fewer repeat contacts
- Fewer unsupported credits
- Fewer failed field visits
- Faster first responses
- Cleaner handoffs
- Evidence before escalation
- One priority view across systems

## What They Do Not Want

- Another blank dashboard
- A chatbot that hides decisions
- A generic automation canvas
- A platform migration
- Compliance theatre
- AI that sends risky messages without review

## Site Direction

Warm, practical, department-manager friendly.

Visual language:

- Coffee-toned control room
- 2D office map
- Desks for departments
- Meeting room for complex escalation
- Kitchen as low-risk queued work
- Review Gate as the human checkpoint

Tone:

```text
Calm office. Clear ownership. No mystery handoffs.
```
