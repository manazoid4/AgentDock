# AgentDock Competitive Research

Updated: 6 May 2026

## Summary

This research reviews 42 competitors across enterprise ITSM, CX AI, contact centre AI, RPA, workflow automation, and agent frameworks.

The market is crowded, but the gap is still clear:

```text
Suites automate inside their own ecosystem.
Builders create agents.
AgentDock gives service managers one warm control room for risky work across systems.
```

## Positioning

AgentDock should not position as another AI builder.

The better wedge:

- Neutral control room for service agents
- Warm operations floor, not cold automation console
- Human approval before sensitive action
- Complaint clocks and SLA risk made visible
- Works around ServiceNow, Zendesk, Jira, finance, field ops, and contact-centre platforms
- Fast pilot path for department managers who need proof before procurement theatre

Sharp line:

```text
Run every service agent from one dock. See what it did. Control what it can touch.
```
