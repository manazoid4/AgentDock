## First Demo Narrative

Lead with complaint management:

```text
A high-priority complaint lands in ServiceNow.
Complaint Agent classifies it as critical.
Response Agent drafts the first reply from approved policy.
Review Gate asks a human to approve, reject, or modify.
AgentDock logs the action and updates ServiceNow.
```

Then show expansion:

- Finance Agent for invoice disputes and payment follow-ups.
- Sales Agent for lead qualification and proposals.
- HR Agent for policy and onboarding queries.
- IT Support Agent for service desk triage.
- Report Agent for scheduled management packs.

## Copy Rules

Say:

- Keep your systems.
- Agents do the work.
- You see and control everything.
- Nothing critical happens without your say-so.
- Audit trails that satisfy auditors on day one.

Avoid:

- Autonomous
- Replace your systems
- AI-powered without a concrete action
- Abstract charts instead of the product
